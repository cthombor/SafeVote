# UK Labour Party Leader 2010

These are the ballots cast by Labour MPs and MEPs in an election of
their party's leader in 2010, as published by the Manchester Guardian.
The names of the electors have been suppressed in this file, but are
available at rangevoting.org, along with extensive commentary on the
election.

## Usage

``` r
uk_labour_2010
```

## Format

A data frame with 266 observations and 5 candidates.
